    Digital Logic Trainer Board — Operation & Wiring Guide

    1. Breadboard Electrical Connectivity Rules

    Just like a real solderless breadboard:

    • Top & Bottom Power Rails: All holes along the red + rail are connected horizontally. All holes along the blue/black − rail are connected horizontally. Connect trainer +5V VCC and GND terminals to these rails first.
    • Terminal Strips (Columns 1–63): In each column, holes A–B–C–D–E are electrically connected together, and holes F–G–H–I–J are electrically connected together.
    • Center IC Trench: Separates rows A–E from rows F–J so DIP IC pins on opposite sides do not short together.

    2. Placing ICs & Connecting Jumper Wires

    • Placing ICs: Drag any 74-series IC from the left drawer onto the breadboard center trench (or click + Place). Pin 1 is always at the bottom-left near the semicircular notch.
    • Click-to-Click Wiring: Click any breadboard hole, switch terminal (SW1–SW8), LED terminal (LED1–LED8), 7-segment pin, or +5V/GND jack, then click the destination hole/terminal.
    • Drag-to-Connect Wiring: You can also press and drag directly from one terminal/hole to another.
    • Deleting Wires: Right-click any jumper wire, or enable Cut Wire mode in the top toolbar and click a wire.

    3. Real Hardware Power & Fault Protection

    • Every 74-series IC requires valid VCC (+5V) and GND (0V) connections on its datasheet power pins (typically Pin 14/16 = VCC and Pin 7/8 = GND; note 7483 uses Pin 5 = VCC, Pin 12 = GND; 7476 uses Pin 5 = VCC, Pin 13 = GND; 7490/7493 use Pin 5 = VCC, Pin 10 = GND).
    • If +5V is shorted directly to GND, or two IC outputs with opposing logic levels are shorted together, the FAULT alarm LED illuminates and the Diagnostics panel highlights the exact node conflict.
